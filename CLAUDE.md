Site for soflocashhomebuyers.com (Jim chose this domain on Nov 18, 2026; blackburncapitalpartners.com is not registered). Blackburn Capital Partners LLC (Florida) buys older single-family homes that need work, for cash, on its own or together with investment partners. Sister site to blackburn.capital (hotel acquisitions), which is a separate project. Never mix the two.
